First come, first served

There are only 77 Divine knights. Whoever collects 1,500 $THYRK and redeems first gets one — until they are gone. There is no draw and no waiting list. Divine is earned by effort, not luck: Legendary is the lucky draw, Divine is the reward for collecting.

How to redeem

  1. Earn $THYRK: 20 per mint (free and paid), 20 / 50 / 120 / 300 for burning a Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary.
  2. Collect 1,500 $THYRK in your wallet.
  3. Ask your agent to redeem — the 1,500 $THYRK is burned and you receive 1 Divine knight.
  4. Your Divine artwork is revealed only to you, its owner. It is never shown on this site.
