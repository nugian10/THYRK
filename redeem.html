How to redeem

  1. Earn $THYRK: 20 per paid mint, 20 / 50 / 120 / 300 for burning a Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary, or 20 for reposting the official THYRK post on X (max 1 per wallet).
  2. Collect 1,500 $THYRK in your wallet.
  3. Redeem here — the 1,500 $THYRK is burned and you receive 1 Divine knight.
  4. Your Divine artwork is revealed only to you, its owner. It is never shown on this site.
