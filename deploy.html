Deploy the Contracts

Deploy the $THYRK token and the THYRK NFT contract to BNB Smart Chain from your own browser wallet. No private key, no seed phrase — ever. Your wallet signs each transaction after you click.

Safety first. This page will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Only connect your browser wallet (MetaMask). If any page or person asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam — stop immediately. Deployment costs real BNB (see the estimate in step 3 before you sign anything).
Two contracts, in order. (1) ThyrkToken ($THYRK, ERC-20, cap 777,777) is deployed first. (2) ThyrkNFT (ERC-721, 7,777 + 77 Divine) is deployed second and needs the token address. The deployer wallet only pays gas — the owner of both contracts is Bryan's My Wallet (0x0262e8e5E4584D8AB840fD950a6E2E0A414d4097), set as a constructor argument, while mint payments go to the Binance Agentic Wallet (revenue). Steps 6–7 (setMinter, activate) are owner-only: connect My Wallet to run them here.
