Free Mint — Automatic

Your first mint per wallet is free, automatically — 1 per wallet, with no total cap. There is nothing to choose and nothing to sign up for: the free mint is simply your first mint.

The free mint draws from the full deck — it can be a Common, Rare, Epic or even a Legendary. And it earns the full 20 $THYRK, exactly like a paid mint.

You can also mint several at once: bulk mint lets you mint up to 30 knights in a single transaction — 1 free + 29 paid.

FAQ

Can I mint from this website?

No. THYRK is agent-only. Only an AI agent whose wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID can mint, and only after you confirm the exact price in your wallet.

How much does a mint cost?

The mint price is not shown on this site. Your agent reads the exact price from the contract for your next mint and shows it in your wallet confirmation before you sign. The price depends on how many times your wallet has minted.

Is the first mint free?

Yes. Your first mint per wallet is free, automatically — 1 per wallet, with no total cap. It draws from the full deck, so it can even be a Legendary, and it earns the full 20 $THYRK.

Can I mint more than one at a time?

Yes. Bulk minting lets you mint up to 30 knights in one transaction — 1 free + 29 paid.

What is Divine?

Divine is a special rarity of 77 unique 1-of-1 knights. It cannot be minted. You redeem it with 1,500 $THYRK. Its artwork is hidden on this site and revealed only to its owner.

What is $THYRK for?

Three clear uses: redeem a Divine (1,500), redeem a mint (100, max 3 per wallet), and upgrade knights (3 Common + 50, 3 Rare + 100, 3 Epic + 200). You earn it from minting (20 $THYRK per mint, free and paid) and from burning knights.

Is mint still open?

Yes — minting is live. $THYRK features (burn, redeem, upgrade, token mint) are live too.
