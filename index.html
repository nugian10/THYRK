7,777 mintable knights in four rarities, drawn from a shuffled deck. Every knight is a 16-bit pixel artwork. Common, Rare and Epic each have 5 designs, built from the five reference knights. Free Mint — Your Choice

There are 777 free mints in total, first come first served, 1 per wallet. A free mint always gives a Common knight — never Rare, Epic or Legendary.

Your free mint is your choice: it is not automatically your first mint. You decide when to use it, at any point within your 30 mints per wallet. A free mint earns no $THYRK at mint time — but the Common card can still be burned for 20 $THYRK, like any Common.

You can also mint several at once: bulk mint lets you mint multiple knights in a single transaction, up to your 30-per-wallet limit.

FAQ

Can I mint from this website?

No. THYRK is agent-only. Only an AI agent whose wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID can mint, and only after you confirm the exact price in your wallet.

How much does a mint cost?

The mint price is not shown on this site. Your agent reads the exact price from the contract for your next mint and shows it in your wallet confirmation before you sign. The price depends on how many times your wallet has minted.

Is the first mint free?

There are 777 free mints in total, 1 per wallet, Common only — but it is your choice when to use it, not automatically your first mint.

Can I mint more than one at a time?

Yes. Bulk minting lets you mint several knights in one transaction, up to 30 mints per wallet in total.

What is Divine?

Divine is a special rarity of 77 unique 1-of-1 knights. It cannot be minted. You redeem it with 2,000 $THYRK. Its artwork is hidden on this site and revealed only to its owner.

What is $THYRK for?

Three clear uses: redeem a Divine (2,000), redeem a mint (100, max 3 per wallet), and upgrade knights (3 Common + 50, 3 Rare + 100, 3 Epic + 200). You earn it from paid mints, from burning knights, and from reposting the official THYRK post on X.

Where does the mint payment go?

Directly to the revenue wallet (the Binance Agentic Wallet) in the same transaction. It is never held in the contract.

Is mint live yet?

No. The contract is not deployed yet. This site is live first; minting opens after the contract is deployed and verified.
