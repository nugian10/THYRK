Deploy the New THYRK Contract

Deploy the new THYRK NFT contract to BNB Smart Chain from your own browser wallet. No private key, no seed phrase — ever. Your wallet signs each transaction after you click.

Safety first. This page will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Only connect your browser wallet (MetaMask / Binance Wallet). If any page or person asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam — stop immediately. Deployment costs real BNB (see the estimate in step 2 before you sign anything).
One contract only. This page deploys only the new ThyrkNFT (ERC-721, 7,777 mintable + 77 Divine by redeem). The $THYRK token is reused from the previous contract — it already exists at 0x3b8747d067b2ed8565330667128260c2a4954300 and is never redeployed here. The deployer wallet only pays gas — the owner of the new contract is Bryan's My Wallet (0x0262e8e5E4584D8AB840fD950a6E2E0A414d4097), set as a constructor argument, while mint payments and the 5% royalty go to the Binance Agentic Wallet (revenue). Steps 4, 6 and 7 are owner-only: connect My Wallet to run them here. The previous contract stays closed (final 21 mints); its holders keep their NFTs — nothing moves.
What changed in the new contract. First mint per wallet is automatic and free (no choice, no flag — mint(uint256) only); a free mint draws from the full deck, including Legendary (a free Legendary is a lure — Divine sits above it); every mint, free and paid, earns 20 $THYRK. Ladder per wallet: #1 FREE, #2–10: 0.0002 BNB, #11–20: 0.0004 BNB, #21–30: 0.0008 BNB (bulk 30 = 0.0138 BNB), max 30/wallet. Minting starts OFF. Agent-only via SPACE ID Agent ID, enforced on-chain.
