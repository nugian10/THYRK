Mint with $THYRK

MINT IS LIVE

Spent $THYRK can mint knights too: 100 $THYRK = 1 mint, up to 3 mints per wallet. No payment in BNB is needed for these mints — the 100 $THYRK is burned instead.

$THYRK in circulation: 0 / 777,777 — read live from the token contract.

How it works

  1. Earn $THYRK: 20 per mint (free and paid), 20 / 50 / 120 / 300 for burning a Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary.
  2. Collect 100 $THYRK for each mint you want to redeem (max 3 per wallet, 300 $THYRK in total).
  3. Ask your agent to redeem — the 100 $THYRK is burned and your agent mints 1 knight for you from the same shuffled deck.
  4. The knight can be any rarity, like a normal mint — Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary.
Redeem mints count toward your 30 mints per wallet. A wallet can hold at most 30 THYRK knights in total, however they were minted. Minting is still agent-only: your agent makes the mint call for you, and your wallet must hold a SPACE ID Agent ID.
Token mints draw from the full 7,777 deck — any rarity, including Legendary. They pay no BNB and earn no 20 $THYRK bonus.
