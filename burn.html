Burn Knights for $THYRK

MINT IS LIVE

Burning means destroying a knight to get $THYRK in return. A knight you do not want — a duplicate Common, for example — becomes $THYRK you can spend on a Divine, a token mint, or an upgrade.

Burning is live with the new contract. Burning a knight mints $THYRK to your wallet. A burned knight also makes room: burning lowers the total supply of knights in existence.
Burning is permanent — the NFT is gone forever. A burned knight can never be recovered, traded, or unburned. Only burn knights you are sure you do not want to keep.

Divine cannot be burned. Only Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary knights can be burned.

Free mints earn $THYRK too

Your free first mint earns the full 20 $THYRK, exactly like a paid mint. And any Common you hold — free or paid — burns for the same 20 $THYRK.

Example

Burn 5 Common knights: 5 × 20 = 100 $THYRK — exactly enough for 1 token mint on the Token Mint page.
