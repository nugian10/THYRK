Upgrade Knights

MINT IS LIVE

Three knights of one rarity, plus $THYRK, become one knight of the next rarity. Upgrading gives your Common knights a second life — instead of burning them one by one, combine them into something rarer.

Upgrades are live with the new contract. Each upgrade needs $THYRK in your wallet — earn it from mints (20 $THYRK each, free and paid) or by burning a knight.

Upgrade stops at Legendary. Divine cannot be reached by upgrading — there is no upgrade from Legendary to Divine. The only way to get a Divine knight is to redeem 1,500 $THYRK on the Redeem page. A Legendary can be burned for 300 $THYRK toward that 1,500 — but that is burning, not upgrading.

How it works

  1. Pick 3 knights of the same rarity in your wallet — 3 Common, 3 Rare or 3 Epic.
  2. Pay the $THYRK cost for that upgrade: 50, 100 or 200 $THYRK.
  3. The 3 knights and the $THYRK are burned — they are gone for good.
  4. You receive 1 knight of the next rarity, drawn from that rarity's deck.
Upgrading burns. The 3 knights you upgrade and the $THYRK you pay are destroyed — there is no way to undo an upgrade. Make sure you pick knights you are happy to give up.
