How to Mint

COMING SOON — contract not deployed yet

THYRK is agent-only. There is no mint button for humans on this site. An AI agent mints for you — and only if your wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID.

Before you mint

  • You need a wallet on BNB Chain with a SPACE ID Agent ID attached to it.
  • You need an AI agent that can call contracts for you (for example the Binance Agentic Wallet agent).
  • Your wallet can mint at most 30 THYRK knights in total.
  • There are 777 free mints in total, 1 per wallet, Common only. Your free mint is your choice — you decide when to use it, at any point within your 30 mints. It is not automatic.

The mint flow

  1. Give your agent the prompt on the home page, or point it to agent-skill.md.
  2. Your agent checks the contract: mint is open, your wallet holds an Agent ID, and you are below 30 mints.
  3. Your agent reads the exact price for your next mint from the contract and shows it in your wallet confirmation before you sign. The price is never shown on this site.
  4. You confirm. Your agent mints — one knight per call, or several at once with bulk mint (one transaction, up to your remaining limit).
  5. Your agent verifies the mint on-chain and shows you your knight: image, name, rarity and traits.
  6. Paid mints earn 20 $THYRK each. Mint payment goes directly to the revenue wallet in the same transaction.

After you mint

  • Check your knight on the Check page.
  • Burn a knight you do not want to earn $THYRK: Common 20, Rare 50, Epic 120, Legendary 300.
  • Collect 2,000 $THYRK to redeem a Divine — 77 unique 1-of-1 knights, revealed only to their owner.
  • Or spend $THYRK on a redeem mint (100 $THYRK, max 3 per wallet) or on upgrades (3 Common + 50, 3 Rare + 100, 3 Epic + 200).
