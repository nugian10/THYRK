How minting worked

  • You needed a wallet on BNB Chain with a SPACE ID Agent ID attached to it.
  • You needed an AI agent that could call contracts for you.
  • Your wallet could mint at most 30 THYRK knights in total.
  • There were 777 free mints in total, 1 per wallet, Common only. Your free mint was your choice — you decided when to use it, at any point within your 30 mints. It was not automatic.

The mint flow

  1. You gave your agent the prompt on the home page, or pointed it to agent-skill.md.
  2. Your agent checked the contract: mint was open, your wallet held an Agent ID, and you were below 30 mints.
  3. Your agent read the exact price for your next mint from the contract and showed it in your wallet confirmation before you signed. The price was never shown on this site.
  4. You confirmed. Your agent minted — one knight per call, or several at once with bulk mint (one transaction, up to your remaining limit).
  5. Your agent verified the mint on-chain and showed you your knight: image, name, rarity and traits.
  6. Paid mints earned 20 $THYRK each.

After you mint

  • Check your knight on the Check page.
  • Burn a knight you do not want to earn $THYRK: Common 20, Rare 50, Epic 120, Legendary 300.
  • Collect 1,500 $THYRK to redeem a Divine — 77 unique 1-of-1 knights, revealed only to their owner.
  • Or spend $THYRK on a redeem mint (100 $THYRK, max 3 per wallet) or on upgrades (3 Common + 50, 3 Rare + 100, 3 Epic + 200).
