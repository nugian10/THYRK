How to Mint

✦ MINT IS LIVE

Minted – / 7,777

The new THYRK contract is deployed and mint is LIVE. When it opens, minting will be agent-only. There is no mint button for humans on this site. An AI agent mints for you — and only if your wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID.

How minting will work

  • You need a wallet on BNB Chain with a SPACE ID Agent ID attached to it.
  • You need an AI agent that can call contracts for you.
  • Your wallet can mint at most 30 THYRK knights in total.
  • Your first mint per wallet is FREE and automatic — no choice to make, no separate free-mint step. It applies to 1 mint per wallet, with no total cap on free mints.
  • The free mint draws from the full deck, including Legendary — a free mint can draw any tier: Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary.
  • Bulk mint: up to 30 knights in 1 transaction. A 30-mint bulk costs 0.0138 BNB in total (1 free + 29 paid).
  • Price ladder per wallet: #1 FREE, #2–10 0.0002 BNB, #11–20 0.0004 BNB, #21–30 0.0008 BNB. The exact price is always read from the contract at mint time and shown in your wallet confirmation before you sign. The price is never shown as a static number on this site.
  • Every mint — free and paid — earns 20 $THYRK.

The mint flow

  1. You give your agent the prompt below, or point it to agent-skill.md.
  2. Your agent checks the contract: mint is open, your wallet holds an Agent ID, and you are below 30 mints.
  3. Your agent reads the exact price for your mint from the contract with quoteBulk(wallet, quantity) and shows it in your wallet confirmation before you sign.
  4. You confirm. Your agent calls mint(uint256 quantity) — one knight, or up to 30 at once in a single transaction. There is no separate free-mint argument: the first mint per wallet is free automatically.
  5. Your agent verifies the mint on-chain and shows you your knight: image, name, rarity and traits.
  6. Each mint earns 20 $THYRK, free mints included.

After you mint

  • Check your knight on the Check page.
  • Burn a knight you do not want to earn $THYRK: Common 20, Rare 50, Epic 120, Legendary 300.
  • Collect 1,500 $THYRK to redeem a Divine — 77 unique 1-of-1 knights, revealed only to their owner.
  • Or spend $THYRK on a token mint (100 $THYRK, max 3 per wallet) or on upgrades (3 Common + 50, 3 Rare + 100, 3 Epic + 200).
